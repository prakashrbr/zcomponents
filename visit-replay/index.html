Visit Replay

Replays a recorded visit next to the note the AI drafted from it. Every part of the note shows where it came from: the lines in the transcript and the moment in the audio.

Goal: let providers check the AI draft against what was actually said, quickly, before they sign.

When To Use

  • A visit was recorded and the AI drafted the note.
  • The provider wants to check a section or a single statement against the conversation.
  • Part of the note is based on audio that wasn't clear.

When Not To Use

  • There's no recording, or no consent to keep one.
  • Editing the note. Use the note editor; this view is for review.
  • Live transcription during the visit.

Waveform

The recording is shown as bars, coloured by what happened in that part of the visit:

  • Provider speech used in the note
  • Patient speech used in the note
  • Said, but not in the note
  • Not clear

Tags under the waveform mark each discussion (history, medications, examination…). Hover a tag to see its name; select it to jump there and play that section.

AI Answer & Provenance

  • The selected section's draft appears above, and is broken into simple sentences below.
  • Selecting a sentence highlights the transcript lines it came from, links them, marks the phrase in the draft, and plays those lines.
  • Selecting a transcript line plays it, and selects the sentence that uses it.
  • Sentences based on unclear audio, or with no source at all, are flagged.
Every sentence in the note should link to at least one line of the conversation. The provider decides; the replay only shows the evidence.

Behavior

  • Clips stop at the end of the source, so the provider hears just the evidence.
  • Play continues freely, and the view follows the recording from section to section.
  • Nothing in the note changes here. Done marks the review as complete; Cancel closes without changes.

Clinical Timeline

When the patient refers to something in their past ("the sinus thing was about three weeks ago"), the timeline shows their record around it and the event the AI matched.

  • It follows the audio: the mention shown is the latest one heard at the playhead.
  • Events are plotted by type: ● encounter, ■ lab, ◆ medication. The green dot is this visit.
  • The matched event is ringed in blue, with its details below.
  • Not this event moves to the AI's next suggestion, which the provider can confirm with Use this event. When every suggestion is ruled out, the mention stays unlinked.
  • Selecting any other event previews it without linking it. Esc returns to the match.
  • Switches hide event types; the linked event always stays visible.

Accessibility

  • The waveform is a slider: arrows seek 5 seconds (Shift: 15), Space plays or pauses.
  • Tags, sentences and transcript lines each take one Tab stop; arrow keys move within them.
  • Status never relies on colour alone: lines say "Not clear" or "Not in note".
  • Selections are announced, including the times of the sources.
  • Animations are removed under reduced motion.

Anatomy

Numbers are placed from the rendered component, so they follow each design system's layout.

    States

    Waveform, tag, sentence and transcript states. All colours come from the shared palette, so they're the same in every design system.

    Light and Dark

    The current design system in both themes. These follow the design system switch but ignore the theme switch.
